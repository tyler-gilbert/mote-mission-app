## Commander Role

You will be assigned team members.

Startup Tasks

- Assign roles to team members. You need at least one team member per role. Some roles can have multiple members.
- Ensure team members have color matching name tags with individual names and team names.

Training Tasks

- Ensure your team has everything they need to succeed.
- Ensure all team members are able to test their parts.

Operational Tasks

- Go/No Go decision. Decide when you are ready to execute the mission.
- Mission debrief
